Mount Fuji

Recommendations for places to visit around Mount Fuji!

Mount Fuji is the highest mountain in Japan and one of the country's most famous landmarks. It is known for its beautiful cone shape, snowy peak and stunning natural scenery. Mount Fuji is a popular destination for hiking, sightseeing and photography.

Natural Places

1. Lake Kawaguchi

A beautiful lake offering amazing views of Mount Fuji.

Why visit? Photography and relaxing by the lake.

2. Arakurayama Sengen Park

A famous park with a pagoda and beautiful views of Mount Fuji.

Why visit? Stunning scenery and cherry blossoms in spring.


Famous Places

3. Chureito Pagoda

A five-storey pagoda overlooking the town and Mount Fuji.

Why visit? One of Japan's most iconic photo spots.

4. Mount Fuji 5th Station

A popular starting point for hikers climbing Mount Fuji.

Why visit? Mountain views and hiking experiences.


Adventure and Entertainment

5. Fuji-Q Highland

An exciting amusement park with thrilling roller coasters and attractions.

Why visit? Fun rides and incredible views of Mount Fuji.

6. Oshino Hakkai

A traditional village famous for its clear spring-water ponds and old houses.

Why visit? Traditional Japanese culture and beautiful scenery.
