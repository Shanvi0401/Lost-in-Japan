Hiroshima

Recommendations for places to visit in Hiroshima!

Hiroshima is a historic city in Japan known for its tragic past and its message of peace. Today, it is a vibrant city with beautiful parks, historic landmarks, delicious food and scenic islands. Hiroshima offers visitors a chance to learn about history, experience Japanese culture and enjoy beautiful natural scenery.

Historical Places

1. Hiroshima Peace Memorial Park

A peaceful park dedicated to the victims of the atomic bombing in 1945.

Why visit? Learn about history and reflect on the importance of peace.

2. Atomic Bomb Dome

A historic building preserved as a reminder of the atomic bombing.

Why visit? Understand Hiroshima's history and its message of peace.


Famous Places

3. Hiroshima Castle

A reconstructed Japanese castle surrounded by beautiful grounds and a moat.

Why visit? Discover traditional Japanese architecture and history.

4. Itsukushima Shrine

A famous shrine on Miyajima Island, known for its large red torii gate over the water.

Why visit? Enjoy stunning coastal scenery and Japanese traditions.


Food and Entertainment

5. Okonomimura

A popular food spot with several restaurants serving Hiroshima-style okonomiyaki.

Why visit? Taste a famous local dish made with layers of noodles, cabbage and other ingredients.

6. Shukkeien Garden

A traditional Japanese garden with ponds, bridges, trees and peaceful walking paths.

Why visit? Relax in nature and enjoy beautiful Japanese garden design.
