Tokyo

Recommandations for places to visit in Tokyo!

Tokyo is the capital city of Japan and one of the world's most exciting cities. It is famous for its bright neon lights, modern skyscrapers, historic temples and beautiful cherry blossoms. Tokyo offers a unique mix of traditional Japanese culture and modern technology.

Traditional Places

1. Sensō-ji Temple

A famous Buddhist temple known for its giant red lantern.

Why visit? Japanese history and culture.

2. Meiji Jingu Shrine

A peaceful shrine surrounded by a forest.

Why visit? Nature and Japanese traditions.

Famous Places

3. Shibuya Crossing

A world-famous crossing surrounded by bright lights and shops.

Why visit? Shopping and photography.

4. Tokyo Skytree

A 634-metre tower offering amazing city views.

Why visit? Panoramic views of Tokyo.

Shopping and Entertainment

5. Akihabara

A district famous for anime, gaming and electronics.

Why visit? Anime fans and gamers.

6. Harajuku

A colourful area known for fashion and Japanese sweets.

Why visit? Shopping and street fashion.
